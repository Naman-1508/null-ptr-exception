and wants to ensure that its employees are using strong passwords. IT security team has
been tasked with conducting a password strength assessment to identify weak
passwords that may pose a security risk. The IT security team decides to use a
password cracking tool, to perform the password strength assessment. Plan is to target
the company's internal systems, including FTP, SSH. By using Hydra password
cracking tool perform a password strength assessment, so that ABC Corp's IT sec team
was able to identify & address weaknesses in their network's authentication.
